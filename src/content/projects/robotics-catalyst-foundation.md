## Why it exists

Starting a robotics team is expensive: about $2,500 for an FTC team and $800 for a FIRST LEGO League team in the first year alone. The foundation exists to cut that first-year barrier so more students get to compete.

## What it does

- **Funding**: direct grants to new and existing teams across FLL, FTC, VEX, and NHRL, including two FIRST LEGO League teams in Wilton
- **Fiscal sponsorship**: teams can operate under the foundation's 501(c)(3), with debit cards and tax-deductible donation pages, run through [Robolyst](/projects/robolyst)
- **Robolyst**: the foundation builds and operates Robolyst, free for every team

## Results

Over $14,000 raised and 5 teams funded so far, reaching over 1,500 students.
