## The team

Singularity Technology is a FIRST Tech Challenge team based out of the Wilton Library. I joined in 2023 and have led the team as co-captain for the past 2 years.

Focused on team management and sustainability, I've worked to create a pipeline of robotics programs in Wilton, CT.

## Results

The team has qualified for and competed in the FTC World Championship(24-25) and the FTC Premier event "Run for the Robots"(25-26).

[Team Awards](https://robolyst.org/team/ftc/7034/awards) during my tenure:
* Inspire Award x5
* Connect Award x3
* Sustain Award x1
* Innovate Award x1
* Motivate Award x1
