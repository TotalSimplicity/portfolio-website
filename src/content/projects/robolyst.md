## Why I built it

FTC teams juggle rankings, match data, tasks, budgets, and scouting across a dozen scattered tools. Robolyst puts all of it in one place, free for every team.

## What it does

- Season HQ: tasks, schedules, pit and pre-match checklists, docs, finances, inbox, scouting, outreach tracking, and public team websites
- Fiscal sponsorship so teams can manage finances under the foundation's 501(c)(3)
- Discord/slack integration and a mobile app experience
- Directory and world rankings for 25,000+ teams across 10,000+ events
- Live match results pulled from FIRST's Events API, with 190,000+ matches recorded
- Home-grown statistics such as "Schedule-adjusted Luck" & "Strength" calculated for every team
- Match predictions


## Tech Stack:
* SvelteKit Project
* PostgreSQL Database
* Docker containerized server
* Resend for email
* Gemini & Jev for auto-tagged team entries
* Stripe for card issuing & donations

## Results

700+ users and 160+ teams use the platform, with 161 teams actively running their season in HQ within 2 months of launch.
